About

Pipelines by day, silicon by choice

I'm a software engineer based in Bangalore, currently on Fujitsu Research of India's MONAKA team, building the data and performance layer for a next-generation ARM-based chip aimed at data centers. Before that, I spent two years at Samsung R&D Institute Bangalore re-architecting large-scale data lakes and ETL pipelines, and started out at HSBC building data-sync tooling between AWS and on-premise storage.

Outside of pipeline work, I contribute ARM SVE optimizations to Milvus's Knowhere vector-search engine, co-authored a research poster on accelerating Milvus on ARM presented at ISC-HPC 2026 in Germany, and I'm finishing an M.Tech in AI/ML at BITS Pilani.
